## H2: What an executive virtual assistant handles
Six cards:
1. **Inbox management:** triage, drafts in your voice, follow-up flags, inbox-zero routine.
2. **Calendar and scheduling:** meetings across time zones, buffers, rescheduling, prep notes in each invite.
3. **Travel and logistics:** flights, hotels, itineraries, visas and documents checklist.
4. **Meetings and follow-ups:** agendas, notes, action items, chasing owners until done.
5. **Investor, board and client prep:** collecting numbers, preparing decks and updates from your templates.
6. **CRM and business admin:** pipeline updates, reports, document filing, vendor coordination.
Link line: See the full list: [30 tasks to delegate to an executive assistant](/blog/executive-assistant-tasks).
