# Alpha Ahead SEO plan (26 Nov 2026 to 28 Feb 2027)

One consolidated plan built on everything already in this repo (RUNBOOK.md, CHANGELOG.md C1-C37,
keyword-map-2026-11-20.csv, page-map-2026-11-24.csv, the 19 Nov investigation and the 12-hour GSC snapshots).
It does not restart the work; it puts the remaining work in order. RUNBOOK.md stays the operating manual
for each 12-hour run; this file is the 90-day roadmap.

## 2. Where it stands today (first-party data only)

- **Traffic:** 12 clicks, 269 impressions since visibility returned on 16 Nov (it was zero from Jan to mid-Nov 2026). Daily impressions grew from 4 to about 50.
- **First non-brand wins:** "is cold calling illegal in us" pos 6, "sdr appointment setter" pos 4, industry pages already showing at pos 33-78.
- **Indexing:** most pages indexed fast. Still not indexed: `/about` (crawled, not indexed), `/blog/onboard-virtual-assistant-7-days` and `/blog/bookkeeping-virtual-assistant-checklist` (discovered, not crawled).
- **Leads:** form now delivers (C1). Real lead count per month is not yet reported.

### Biggest gaps (ranked by impact)
1. **No authority / off-site footprint.** About 2,200 referring domains, ~99% spam (AS 0-10, paid-link anchors). No Clutch, GoodFirms, Google Business Profile or press mentions. This is the main ceiling on rankings for commercial terms.
2. **No analytics.** GA4 code is ready (`src/utils/track.ts`) but `REACT_APP_GA4_ID` is not set in the deploy workflow, so no GA4 data is collected. We cannot measure organic sessions or leads by page.
3. **No proof on money pages.** No prices, testimonials or named results; competitors that rank show all three.
4. **Thin E-E-A-T signals.** Blog posts have no named author with a bio; `/about` is not indexed.
5. **Translations are invisible to Google.** Spanish, French, Urdu and Arabic exist only as a client-side language switch (no separate URLs, no hreflang), so Google indexes English only. Fine for now; matters for UAE later.
6. **Housekeeping:** two empty stray files in the repo root (`(` and `{`).

## 3. Strategy in one paragraph

Win the low-competition, high-intent keywords first (P1: KD ≤ 20, industry pages, appointment setting,
bookkeeping VA, cold calling legality), make the money pages convincing (proof, process, FAQs), build a clean
off-site entity (directories, profiles, sister-site links, a few earned mentions) so Google and AI answer engines
trust the brand, and publish one useful post or page per week driven by real GSC queries. Keep strict lanes:
general business VAs, bookkeeping VA, executive/personal VA, offshore (Pakistan) VA, B2B cold calling,
appointment setting, CRM management, Dubai/UAE VAs. No real estate, property management or medical.

### Phase 2: trust and E-E-A-T (December)
- **Author system:** a real author (name, role, photo, LinkedIn) on every blog post; `/team/<name>` or an author box with `Person` schema; `dateModified` shown.
- **`/about`:** founders, year founded, offices with photos, how vetting works, sister brands. Goal: indexed.
- **Proof (owner supplies, we publish):** 2-3 client results with permission, real testimonials (with `Review` only if they are on the page and genuine), pricing ranges in USD/AED or "from" prices.
- **Organization schema:** own phone number (separate from Zingo), `sameAs` to every new profile from Phase 3.
- **Case studies:** turn each owner-approved result into a case study page linked from its service page.

### Phase 3: off-site authority (ongoing from December, the biggest lever)
Targets from RUNBOOK: 8 / 18 / 30 clean referring domains at day 30 / 60 / 90.
1. **Entity profiles (week 1-3):** Google Business Profile for St. Petersburg (only if the address qualifies; virtual offices do not) and Dubai (only if staffed), LinkedIn company page refresh, Clutch, GoodFirms, Crunchbase, Trustpilot, Upwork agency, DesignRush, Sortlist.
2. **UAE citations:** Dubai Chamber, Yellow Pages UAE, Connect.ae, other UAE business directories (exact same name/address/phone everywhere).
3. **Sister sites:** contextual links (not footer-only) from zingoassist.us, alphamediassist.com and learnwithalpha.com to the matching Alpha Ahead pages.
4. **Reviews:** ask every current client for a Clutch or Google review (aim 5+ by day 90).
5. **Earned mentions:** answer journalist requests (Qwoted, Featured, Help a B2B Writer) on outsourcing, remote work, VA hiring; 1 guest article per month on small business / outsourcing sites; partner pages (QuickBooks/Xero ProAdvisor directories if staff qualify, CRM partner listings).
6. **Linkable asset (January):** "Virtual assistant cost 2027" data page with sourced wage floors and a simple cost calculator; pitch it to small business blogs.
7. **Never:** paid links, link packages, PBNs. The existing spam profile is the reason for the disavow.

- Keep the deploy checks green; add new pages to the route list checks when created.
- Every build: `python3 scripts/audit-seo.py build` (one H1, unique titles <= 60, descriptions <= 155, no raw markdown).

## 7. Rules that stay in force
- Lanes: no US real estate / property management (Zingo), no medical (Alpha Medi Assist).
- No invented prices, results, testimonials, tools or rankings. Owner supplies facts; we publish them.
- 7-day rule: a page edited in the last 7 days only gets title/meta/FAQ changes.
- No em dashes, American English, one H1, FAQ schema matches visible FAQs.
- No Google Indexing API; IndexNow + sitemap only.
