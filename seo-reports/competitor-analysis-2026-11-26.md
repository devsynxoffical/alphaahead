## 3. Patterns competitors share (from search snippets)
- Task lists per role (invoicing, reconciliations, CRM updates, calendar, renewals).
- Hiring model options: part-time / full-time, US-based / global, bilingual.
- A visible "from" price or pricing model (VA Masters from $7.50/h; appointment setting per minute / per appointment / setup fee).
- Vetting and security claims (interviews, NDAs), no minimum contract or trial.
- Directory presence (Clutch, DesignRush, Outsource Accelerator, SHRM vendor directory) with reviews.

## 4. Gaps Alpha Ahead can use
1. **Pricing transparency:** we are quote-only. At minimum explain pricing models and what affects price; ideally owner-approved "from" prices.
2. **Directory presence:** Clutch (US VA, AE Dubai VA, appointment setting), DesignRush, GoodFirms, TechBehemoths, Outsource Accelerator, SHRM vendor directory. These rank on page 1 themselves and pass trust.
3. **"Best of" list inclusion (outreach):** Small Business Trends (25 VA companies), Reply.io (best cold calling agencies), Belkins (best appointment setting companies), dev.to UAE VA list.
4. **Executive VA:** marketplaces dominate, so an agency page with managed backup cover, NDAs and vetting is a real differentiator.
5. **Insurance:** human VA vs AI agent comparison is missing from agency pages.
6. **Small business VA:** guide-style content (readiness, task list, trial week) like the US Chamber pages, plus the agency offer.
7. **Cold calling:** proof of call quality (sample script, reporting screenshot, recording policy) and contract flexibility.
